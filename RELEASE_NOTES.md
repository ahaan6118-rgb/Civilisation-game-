## Civilisation Idle (alpha)

An early playtest build. Grow a tribe from wood and food up the ages, craft random items with synergies, and invade neighbouring countries.

### Downloads
- **Mac:** `CivilisationIdle-mac.zip`. Unzip, then right-click **Civilisation Idle** and choose **Open** the first time (it isn't notarized by Apple).
- **Windows:** `CivilisationIdle-windows.zip`. Unzip and run `CivilisationIdle.exe`. If SmartScreen warns you, click **More info** then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** `CivilisationIdle-linux.zip`. Unzip and run `CivilisationIdle.x86_64`.

### What's in it
- 15 resources from Wood to Dark Matter, with food spoilage while you play and offline progress while you're away
- Jobs, housing, discoveries, and milestone upgrades
- Artisans and Blacksmiths that craft random relics and consumables, plus hidden synergies
- Warfare and an invasion map: send troops to take over territory

### Known issues
- Balance past the Clay era is unfinished, so later eras may feel too fast or too slow.
- Placeholder graphics: art is coming.
- Upgrade and item flavor text is still empty.

Saves are kept between versions. Use **Wipe Save** in the bottom right to start over.
