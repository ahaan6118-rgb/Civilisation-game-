## Civilisation Idle (alpha)

An early playtest build. Grow a tribe from wood and food up the ages, try to keep them alive, craft random items, and conquer your way up the Kardashev scale.

### Downloads
- **Mac:** `CivilisationIdle-mac.zip`. Unzip, then right-click **Civilisation Idle** and choose **Open** the first time (it isn't notarized by Apple).
- **Windows:** `CivilisationIdle-windows.zip`. Unzip and run `CivilisationIdle.exe`. If SmartScreen warns you, click **More info** then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** `CivilisationIdle-linux.zip`. Unzip and run `CivilisationIdle.x86_64`.

### New in 0.3.0
- **Genuinely brutal.** Everyone dies of old age now, and births only grow with the square root of your population, so deaths catch up fast. Keep health up or your people die young. The Population panel shows births against old-age deaths.
- **IQ fades** unless Elders keep teaching, helps less than before, and every age makes work more dangerous.
- **Disasters scale with the age** you're in: they come faster, kill more and wreck a share of everything you've built. New late-game disasters include Killer Smog, Pandemic, Reactor Meltdown, Containment Breach and Void Rift.
- **Legacy is much harder** to earn, and Legacy upgrades cost far more.
- **Giant war maps.** Every war is fought on a huge openfront-style map with every nation of that tier plus 160+ minor factions. You start small. The target's neighbours gang up on you, and onlookers pile in if you look weak. Scroll to zoom, and right-drag or WASD to move around. You can use up to 3 items per battle.
- **Bulk buy and assign.** Use x1 / x10 / x100 / x1K / x10K / Max for jobs, buildings and commissions, plus a Clear button that takes everyone off their jobs.
- **Stop or resume** Artisans and Blacksmiths whenever you like.
- Synergies are removed for now, to be designed by hand.

### Known issues
- Most icons and backgrounds are still placeholders.
- Upgrade and item flavor text is still empty.
- Balance past the Iron Age varies a lot between runs.

Older saves load, but it's best to **Wipe Save** (bottom right) and start fresh for this version.
