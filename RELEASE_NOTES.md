## Civilisation Idle (alpha)

An early playtest build. Grow a tribe from wood and food up the ages, try to keep them alive, craft random items with game-breaking synergies, and conquer your way up the Kardashev scale.

### Downloads
- **Mac:** `CivilisationIdle-mac.zip`. Unzip, then right-click **Civilisation Idle** and choose **Open** the first time (it isn't notarized by Apple).
- **Windows:** `CivilisationIdle-windows.zip`. Unzip and run `CivilisationIdle.exe`. If SmartScreen warns you, click **More info** then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** `CivilisationIdle-linux.zip`. Unzip and run `CivilisationIdle.x86_64`.

### New in 0.2.0
- **Suffering.** Workers die in job accidents, from illness, starvation and disasters. Raise Tribe IQ with Elders, lessons and schools so they stop dying so stupidly.
- **Morale and health.** Unhappy people work slower and steal; sick towns get plague.
- **24 random events** while you play: droughts, famines, floods, earthquakes, plague, raiders, tax revolts, civil war, mine exhaustion, golden ages, trade caravans and more. Some can be fixed for a price.
- **War and the world map.** Wage war on neighbouring lands, defend when they declare war on you, and climb the Kardashev scale from The Valley to Beyond (Type VII).
- **Rebirth.** End a run for Legacy and buy permanent upgrades that make the next run more bearable.
- **14 named ages**, from the Wood & Bone Age to the Dark Matter Age.
- **A big click button** and the first sprites (resource icons and the axe).
- **Rebalanced pacing** for a brutal first run.

### Known issues
- Most icons and backgrounds are still placeholders.
- Upgrade and item flavor text is still empty.
- Balance past the Iron Age varies a lot between runs.

Saves from 0.1.0 load, but it's best to **Wipe Save** (bottom right) and start fresh for this version.
